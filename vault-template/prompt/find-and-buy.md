# Recipe: find and buy

"Which one should I get, in which size, where is it cheapest?" Research that ends with links; **the owner pays and
orders**, never the session.

## Steps

1. **Use the `shopping-research` skill** (hommy's plugin) when it is installed: it is the full procedure. Without it,
   follow the steps below.
2. **Pin the exact item**: model, version or year, size, colour. An open choice: shortlist from independent reviews
   first, then research the pick and the runner-up.
3. **Size** (sized items): the sizes file in `context/` when the vault has one, the brand's own fit tool, the brand's
   chart, then shop charts. Give one size and the evidence; never add your own margin.
4. **Offers**: open the product pages in a lane browser (`pw-lane`, the `agent-browsers` skill): the owner's usual
   shops (`context/preferences.md`), the big retailers that deliver to the `home` row of `context/places.md`, the
   category specialists and the brand's store. Per page: every colour, the size selector (in stock?), price with
   delivery, delivery time, return terms.
5. **Answer** with a table: shop · colour · size in stock · price incl. delivery · delivery time · returns · link; then
   the size verdict and one pick. Mark each figure **verified** (page opened) or **unverified**.
6. **Stop before payment.** Never add to cart for checkout, log in to pay or order: send the links. Close every lane
   you opened.
7. After the owner buys: note the item, size and date in the vault (`context/`), and the fit when they report it.
